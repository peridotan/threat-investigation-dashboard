# Disclaimer

Threat Investigation is provided for informational and educational purposes.

Relationships shown in the tool reflect the MITRE ATT&CK dataset available at generation time. Counts of Techniques, Software, Campaigns, Actors, Platforms, or Aliases do not represent threat severity, current activity, likelihood of attack, attribution confidence, or complete defensive coverage.

A zero related-entity count means that no direct relationship is recorded in the loaded ATT&CK dataset. It does not establish that no real-world activity, vendor-reported campaign, or other relationship exists.

Entity Resolution distinguishes direct ATT&CK ID / name / alias matches from ATT&CK relationships. Shared Techniques or other similarities are not presented as derived Actor relationships unless explicitly identified as dashboard-derived analysis.

Investigation Lens, Environment Overlay, and Defensive Investigation Lenses are navigation and investigation aids. They are not official MITRE ATT&CK classifications, control mappings, remediation priorities, attack likelihood estimates, or environment-specific risk scores.

Navigation Context received from Security Intelligence Library or passed to Vulnerability Intelligence is context only. It must not be interpreted as an ATT&CK direct relationship or evidence that a Threat Actor used or exploited a specific CVE.

Users should confirm important security decisions against current primary sources and their own environment.
